Read · Indoors · Teacher-led

Mini Circles read

Mini Circles read is about making reading an enjoyable part of everyday life. In a calm, close reading circle, teachers share stories, invite conversation and make space for young people to read, listen and imagine together. There is no test and no one has to read aloud if they would rather listen.

What happens in a reading circle?

  • Choose a story, poem or short text that fits the group.
  • Read aloud, read together or take turns when everyone is ready.
  • Talk, draw or imagine what might happen next.
  • Close with a favourite word, idea or story moment.

Led by teachers

Teachers guide the session and help keep it welcoming and age-appropriate. The group can shape the stories and activities around its interests.

Languages: Local language choices depend on the school and community. Ask the team about the languages used at a nearby circle; don't assume one language fits every group.

Play · Outdoors · Coach-led

Mini Circles play

Mini Circles play helps young people move, learn skills, play as a team and find a game they enjoy. Coaches bring structure and encouragement; activities can adapt to the group, space and equipment available.

Cricket

KFC Mini Cricket activities introduce cricket skills through active, inclusive play.

Soccer

Small-sided games and practice help young players build confidence and teamwork.

More ways to move

Activities can grow with the local group. Ask about the current programme in your area.

Our coaching standard: tough on effort, soft on kids.

We encourage persistence without humiliation. No pressure to be perfect, no child left out, and no result matters more than safety, respect and enjoyment.

Mini League
