Boomerang by Mini Circles

Stories go further together.

Our reading clubs are warm, lively spaces led by teachers. Read aloud, share a story, try a new word and enjoy the moment. There is no test at the end.

  • Reading for pleasure, with time to listen and imagine.
  • Teachers lead the circle and make room for young voices.
  • Languages are shaped by the local group; ask us what is spoken near you.

Splendid Sports by Mini Circles

Find your game. Find your people.

We take the good stuff outside. Coaches lead cricket, soccer and more, with teamwork at the centre and every young person welcome to have a go.

  • KFC Mini Cricket brings cricket skills and game-day fun.
  • Soccer and more, shaped around the local group.
  • Tough on effort, soft on kids. Every time.

Our coaching standard: We challenge effort and protect the joy. No humiliation, no pressure to be perfect, and no child left out of the team.

Two arches. One M.

Built from where we come from.

The M in Mini Circles brings together Mini and Mlungisi Mashinini. Its two arches echo a welcome, a meeting place and a way forward. Red and yellow draw from the bold geometry and colour of Ndebele blankets; the blue frog brings a little curiosity to every circle.

Mini Circles is youth-led: rooted in community, proud of South Africa and open to what young people can make next.

Where do Mini Circles meet?

Mini Circles is currently working with 8 ECDs and 4 schools. Contact the team to ask which reading and sports circles are active in your area.

Who can volunteer?

Coaches, readers, mentors, media and design helpers are welcome to get in touch. All sessions follow the safeguarding standard of two adults present.

Can my organisation partner with Mini Circles?

Yes. Schools, ECDs, libraries, professionals and sponsors can use the forms above to start a conversation.

How are photos and personal information handled?

We publish event photos only with guardian consent, never show children's full names, and do not use tracking pixels or public comments.

Care comes first.

Safeguarding: Two adults at every session. No child accounts, public comments, individual rankings or user uploads.

Privacy: We collect only what is needed to respond to an enquiry. We do not publish children's full names or identifying details. Photos are used only with guardian consent. This site uses no tracking pixels or advertising trackers.

POPIA: You can ask to access, correct or remove personal information shared with us. Contact details for privacy requests will be added when the team email is configured.
