Young people deserve more room to shine

A place to discover what you love.

Not every young person finds their spark in the same place. Some find it in the next page. Some find it on the field. Mini Circles brings those worlds together, with trusted adults creating space for young people to try, grow and belong.

We are a youth-led South African programme grounded in community. We start with what people already have—local knowledge, a shared story, a patch of open space—and build from there.
