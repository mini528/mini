BOOMERANG BY MINI CIRCLES · READ

Story in a circle

  1. Choose a story together. Let anyone pass if they prefer to listen.
  2. Read a page aloud, or take turns with a sentence.
  3. Ask: “What would you do next?” There are no wrong answers.
  4. Close by sharing one word or picture you want to remember.
BOOMERANG BY MINI CIRCLES · READ

Word explorers

  1. Pick a word from today's story.
  2. Say it, sketch it, act it out or find it in another language.
  3. Make a group sentence using the word.
  4. Celebrate the many ways a word can travel.
SPLENDID SPORTS BY MINI CIRCLES · PLAY

Move & make

  1. Start with a gentle warm-up everyone can adapt.
  2. Make small teams and practise one skill with a soft ball.
  3. Try a short game where passing and teamwork count.
  4. Finish with a team cheer for effort and kindness.
SPLENDID SPORTS BY MINI CIRCLES · PLAY

Everyone's in

  1. Choose a simple target, like passing through a cone gate.
  2. Invite each player to choose a role or a way to take part.
  3. Switch roles so everyone gets a turn.
  4. Ask: “Who helped you have a go today?”

Care comes first: Two adults at every session. Follow your organisation's safeguarding guidance, adapt activities to the group, and keep children’s personal information private.
