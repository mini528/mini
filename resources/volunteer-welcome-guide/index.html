Volunteer welcome guide

Bring good energy. Make room for everyone.

Mini Circles brings reading for fun and sport together. Every circle is a chance to listen, have a go and help young people feel welcome.

How we show up

  • Be warm, reliable and encouraging.
  • Keep the focus on fun, effort, curiosity and teamwork.
  • Make activities welcoming and adaptable; no one has to perform or compete.
  • Respect the local community, languages and the lead teacher or coach.
  • Never share a child's full name, contact details or private story.

Safeguarding is everyone's job

Two adults at every session. Do not run a session alone with a child.

Follow the Foundation's safeguarding policy and the session lead's instructions. Use approved communication channels. If something concerns you, speak to the designated safeguarding lead promptly; do not investigate or promise secrecy. Confirm who the lead is before the session begins.

Photos and privacy

Only approved adults may take or publish photos. Check that guardian consent has been recorded before any image is shared. Do not add children's full names, school details, precise locations or identifying captions. Do not upload photos or personal information to this website.

A circle that works

Plan a simple start, one activity everyone can join, and a calm close. Bring the session cards, adapt for the group, notice effort, and ask the teacher or coach if you are unsure.

Our two wings

Mini Circles read is reading: calm, close and led by teachers.
Mini Circles play is play: bold, energetic and led by coaches. Tough on effort, soft on kids.

Start small. Go limitless.
