Mini Circles is a youth-led programme of The Mlungisi Mashinini Foundation. Reading for fun and sport move hand in hand—rooted in community and open to what young people can make next.

Why Mini Circles?

Space to read

Reading for pleasure can build imagination, confidence and a sense of belonging. Boomerang makes time for stories, conversation and local language choices.

Space to play

Sport is a place to move, practise, play as a team and enjoy trying. Splendid Sports leads with encouragement, effort and care.

One circle, two wings.

Mini Circles is the main programme name. These are its two wings, each led by trusted adults with the right experience.

Indoors · Calm and close · Teachers

Boomerang by Mini Circles

Teacher-led reading clubs designed to make stories and reading enjoyable.

Outdoors · Bold and active · Coaches

Splendid Sports by Mini Circles

Coach-led cricket, soccer and other activities, with effort and enjoyment at the heart.

Safeguarding & privacy

Two adults at every session.

Children do not need accounts to take part. The public site has no child profiles, public comments, user uploads or tracking pixels. We never publish children's full names or identifying details. Event photos are used only with guardian consent.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Enquiry forms should collect only the details needed to reply. The site does not collect information about children through accounts or behavioural tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you run?

The programme currently reaches 8 ECDs and 4 schools. Ask the team about current locations and available activities before travelling.

Which languages do you use?

Language choices depend on the local reading group, school and community. The team can confirm what is available in a particular circle.

Who can volunteer?

Coaches, readers, mentors, media and design helpers can express interest. The team will explain its volunteer process and safeguarding expectations.

Can a school, ECD or library join?

Yes. Use the school, ECD and library enquiry form to start a conversation with the team.

How can I support the programme?

Sponsors and partners can discuss backing a term, team or community moment through the partner enquiry form.

How do I ask about privacy or personal information?

Contact the Foundation through its approved contact channel. A direct team email and privacy contact will be added here when confirmed.

Find us

WhatsApp is the main contact and sharing channel. Follow programme updates on Facebook and YouTube.
